# MerchantHub Design Direction

## Theme Name

**Trading floor, softened**

## Intro

MerchantHub is a warm, high-trust marketplace control center for listings, bids, purchases, and last-mile logistics. It should feel operational and lively without becoming noisy: a deep ink navigation rail anchors the system while coral actions and teal logistics signals create a fast, recognizable rhythm.

## Design movement

Editorial commerce dashboard: crisp data hierarchy, calm surfaces, and a sense of live activity. The experience borrows the confidence of a trading floor, softened with cream surfaces, rounded cards, and generous breathing room.

## Core principles

1. Make the next action obvious: bid, ship, assign, review, or restock.
2. Distinguish live state from historical state with restrained but high-contrast signals.
3. Use compact data modules that can scan quickly, with narrative copy only where trust matters.
4. Treat buyers, sellers, admins, and riders as views into the same operational system.

## Color philosophy

- **Deep ink** `#182329`: navigation, headings, and trusted anchors.
- **Warm cream** `#F7F4EE`: application canvas and brand background.
- **Card white** `#FFFEFB`: primary surfaces with a quiet paper-like contrast.
- **Coral** `#F36F5B`: primary action, live auction pulse, and high-salience moments.
- **Teal** `#2D9C8B`: route, delivery, and positive logistics state.
- **Mustard** `#E5B85C`: attention and pending review.
- **Lavender** `#9787D7`: saved items and secondary intelligence.

## Layout paradigm

Use a desktop-first dashboard with a 248px left rail, a top utility row, and a 12-column content grid. Collapse the rail into a bottom tab bar or drawer on narrow screens. Keep the page background warm cream, cards mostly off-white, and borders quiet. Use asymmetry in the overview hero: a large primary live-auction module balanced by a narrow operational pulse column.

## Signature elements

- A coral “LIVE” capsule paired with a subtle pulsing dot.
- Monospace-style numeric emphasis for bids, money, ETAs, and countdowns.
- Masked bidder avatars with initials for auction transparency.
- Thin teal route lines and circular location markers in the Rider Desk map panel.
- Small “why it matters” microcopy beneath KPI values.
- Product imagery presented as editorial crops with soft corners, not glossy stock-card gradients.

## Interaction philosophy

Use optimistic local updates for favorite toggles, tabs, filters, read state, and delivery statuses. Bids and purchase-related actions are confirmation-first with clear demo-state language. Toast feedback should be concise and actionable. Preserve keyboard focus, use semantic buttons, and provide labels/tooltips for icon-only controls.

## Animation

Use short 150–220ms ease-out transitions for tab changes, card hover elevation, badge state updates, and live feed inserts. Keep the live pulse subtle. Avoid autoplaying carousels and large entrance animations.

## Typography system

Use the existing sans stack for interface copy. Set large metric values in a tight, strong weight with tabular numbers where available. Use uppercase overline labels with increased tracking for system state, and keep paragraph text at a relaxed 14–15px for operational readability.

## Brand essence

MerchantHub turns marketplace uncertainty into a clear next move: compare, bid, buy, ship, and deliver.

## Brand voice

Direct, warm, and trustworthy. Prefer “Reserve not met” over vague error copy, “Ready for pickup” over “Processing”, and “Your max bid is protected” over technical proxy-bid language.

## Wordmark / logo

The symbol is a parcel box transforming into an ascending bid arrow. It is built from three filled geometric shapes: a deep ink parcel base, a coral rising arrow, and a teal hinge/route accent. The mark is simple enough to remain clear at 24px and avoids literal text so it can serve as both header symbol and favicon.

## Signature brand color

Coral `#F36F5B`.
